Your data, in plain words.

What TruckBuddy collects while you drive, why it needs it, and how to delete it.

What the app collects

You sign in with Apple or Google; there is no password and no phone number. The app needs an account because your truck profile, your conversation history and your trips are kept in it, so they come back on a new phone and stay the same on every phone you sign in on.

  • account_circle

    Your account

    From Apple or Google we get an ID for you that does not change, your email address and, if you share it, your name. Google sends only a verified email. With Apple you can choose Hide My Email: then we get a relay address that forwards to your inbox, and we never see your real one. Apple shares your name only the first time you sign in. We show the name and email in the app so you can see which account you’re signed in with; we don’t send you marketing email.

  • devices

    Signed-in phones

    For each phone signed in to your account: whether it is an iPhone or an Android phone, when it signed in and when it was last used. That is what lets you sign out of all phones at once.

  • key

    Apple sign-in token

    If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us a token for TruckBuddy. We keep it encrypted and use it for one thing: to ask Apple to remove TruckBuddy from your Apple ID when you delete your account.

  • location_on

    Location

    Your position while you use the app, to find stops, parking and routes near you and to guide you on a route.

  • mic

    Voice

    What you say after you tap the copilot. It is turned into text so the copilot understands the request and can answer out loud.

  • local_shipping

    Truck profile

    Height, length, weight, hazmat, route preferences and, if you give it, your first name. Stored on your phone and in your account on our server, so routes fit your truck on every request and on every phone you sign in on.

  • forum

    Conversation history

    Your recent requests and the copilot’s replies, so it remembers what you were talking about. You can clear it any time in Conversation memory.

  • route

    Trip events

    While a route is active: when it starts, changes or ends, and requests the copilot could not handle, with the truck’s position at that moment. We use them to find and fix problems. If your phone is set up by your fleet in TruckBuddy, the fleet sees your name, truck profile and trip events, never your conversations.

  • bug_report

    Crash reports and app speed

    The error, the app version and the phone model when something breaks, and how long some screens and requests take. They carry the current trip’s ID so we can match them to a problem on a drive. No voice and nothing you said.

  • schedule

    Hours of service

    If you connect your own ELD, its access key stays in your phone’s secure storage and is used only to read your current clocks for a request. If your fleet connects its ELD account in TruckBuddy, our server keeps the fleet’s key encrypted and reads your clocks with it. We don’t keep the clocks.

  • pin

    Access codes

    Testers and app reviewers can sign in with an access code we give them instead of Apple or Google. Then we get no email or name from Apple or Google, and what we store is tied to a random ID for that phone.

We don’t collect contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Who it is shared with

Only with services that do part of the work on your request. None of them may use it for advertising, and we don’t sell your data.

  • neurology

    AI providers, such as OpenAI and Google

    Your voice, transcripts and the place or request you ask about, including map imagery of a destination, to understand you, answer and speak the reply.

  • map

    Map and routing providers

    Locations and truck dimensions, to show the map, search places, find addresses, plan truck routes and check parking at a destination.

  • partly_cloudy_day

    Weather services

    Points along your route, with nothing about you.

  • monitor_heart

    Crash reporting

    The crash reports described above.

  • link

    Your ELD provider

    Only if you or your fleet connect it, to return your hours-of-service clocks.

How long we keep it

Voice recordingsNot kept after the reply
Conversation historyUntil you clear it
Trip events90 days
Your account and what is in itUntil you delete the account
A signed-in phoneUntil you sign out, 180 days unused, or 365 days after sign-in

Your choices

  • Turn off location or microphone access in your phone’s settings.
  • Clear your conversation history in Conversation memory → Clear memory.
  • Sign out on this phone, or on every phone at once, in Your truck → Sign out or Sign out of all phones. Your data stays in your account.
  • Delete your account in Your truck → Delete account and data, as described below, or email us.
  • Disconnect your own ELD in Truck profile at any time.

Deleting your account

Open Your truck (the truck button next to the search bar), scroll to the bottom, tap Delete account and data and confirm twice. The deletion happens right away, with no waiting period, and can’t be undone. Signed in with an access code, the same place has Delete my data, which erases what we store for that phone.

  • Deleted right away: your account with its ID, email and name; your truck profile; your conversation history and what the copilot learned about you; the places you visited; your trip events; and every signed-in phone. Other phones on the account are signed out within about a minute, and the app on this phone starts over like a fresh install.
  • If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke TruckBuddy’s access to your Apple ID.
  • If a fleet set up your phone, your name, your driver record and your trip events are deleted; the fleet’s own trucks and their profiles stay with the fleet. If you are the only owner of a fleet that still has other drivers or a connected ELD account, the app can’t delete your account; email us to close the fleet or hand it over.
  • A deletion does not reach a few places. Our server logs hold your account’s ID and how long requests took, never what you said; those lines stay until our hosting provider’s log retention drops them. Crash reports carry a trip’s ID rather than your account and stay with our crash reporting service. Data that belongs to no driver, such as a parking check of a place, stays. What was already sent to the services listed above to answer a request is handled under their own policies.
  • If you sign in again later with the same Apple or Google account, you start with a new, empty account.

You can also remove TruckBuddy in your Apple ID settings (Sign in with Apple) or in your Google Account (third-party connections). That ends the link at Apple or Google, but it doesn’t delete your TruckBuddy account or its data, and phones already signed in stay signed in. To delete the data, use Delete account and data or email us.

Children and changes

TruckBuddy is made for professional drivers and is not directed at children under 13. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and important changes are announced in the app.
