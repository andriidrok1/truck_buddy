Your data, in plain words.

What TruckBuddy collects while you drive, why it needs it, and how to delete it.

What the app collects

There is no account. The first time you open the app, it gets a random ID, and what we store is tied to that ID, not to your name or phone number.

  • location_on

    Location

    Your position while you use the app, to find stops, parking and routes near you and to guide you on a route.

  • mic

    Voice

    What you say after you tap the copilot. It is turned into text so the copilot understands the request and can answer out loud.

  • local_shipping

    Truck profile

    Height, length, weight, hazmat, route preferences and, if you give it, your first name. Kept on your phone and sent with requests so routes fit your truck.

  • forum

    Conversation history

    Your recent requests and the copilot’s replies, so it remembers what you were talking about. You can clear it any time in Conversation memory.

  • route

    Trip events

    While a route is active: when it starts, changes or ends, and requests the copilot could not handle, with the truck’s position at that moment. We use them to find and fix problems. If your phone is set up by your fleet in TruckBuddy, the fleet sees trip events, never your conversations.

  • bug_report

    Crash reports

    The error, the app version and the phone model when something breaks. No voice and nothing you said.

  • schedule

    Hours of service, if you connect your ELD

    Your ELD access key stays in your phone’s secure storage. It is used only to read your current clocks for a request, and we keep neither the key nor the clocks.

We don’t collect contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Who it is shared with

Only with services that do part of the work on your request. None of them may use it for advertising, and we don’t sell your data.

  • neurology

    AI providers (OpenAI and Google)

    Your voice, transcripts and the place or request you ask about, to understand you and answer.

  • map

    Map and routing providers

    Locations and truck dimensions, to search places, find addresses and plan truck routes.

  • partly_cloudy_day

    Weather services

    Points along your route, with nothing about you.

  • monitor_heart

    Crash reporting

    The crash reports described above.

  • link

    Your ELD provider

    Only if you connect it, to return your hours-of-service clocks.

How long we keep it

Voice recordingsNot kept after the reply
Conversation historyUntil you clear it
Trip events90 days

Your choices

  • Turn off location or microphone access in your phone’s settings.
  • Clear your conversation history in Conversation memory → Clear memory.
  • Delete everything we store for your phone in Truck profile → Delete my data, or email us.
  • Disconnect your ELD in Truck profile at any time.

Children and changes

TruckBuddy is made for professional drivers and is not directed at children under 13. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and important changes are announced in the app.
