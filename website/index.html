How it works

From “Hey Buddy” to a route in one breath.

  1. “Hey Buddy”
    01

    Wake it up

    Say “Hey Buddy” or tap the avatar. The wake word is heard on the phone, so nothing leaves it until you talk to Buddy.

  2. You said
    need a truck stop with showers
    02

    Talk normally

    Buddy shows what it heard, so you know it got you right before it acts.

  3. 9 min
    03

    Buddy picks and routes

    It weighs your truck, the distance and the parking, picks one place and sets the route. The answer is a sentence or two, out loud and on screen.

  4. Go Spoken turnsLive ETA
    04

    Tap Go

    Driving mode takes over: big turn-by-turn, spoken out loud, with your arrival time always in view.

Truck parking

It looks at the lot before you take the exit.

A listing saying “truck parking” isn’t the same as room for 70 feet of rig. Buddy checks a 300 m satellite view around the place to judge whether a 53 ft tractor-trailer can park there, finds the truck entrance, and ends your route at it.

Tell it your truck once. Every route respects it.

Setup takes one sentence. Height, trailer length, weight and hazmat all shape the route, so Buddy steers you clear of low bridges and roads your truck shouldn’t be on. Change it any time by voice.

Breakdown help

Stopped on the shoulder? Say so. Buddy does the calling around.

Tell Buddy what happened. It asks at most one question, then reads reviews, hours and payment options while you deal with the truck. You get a short spoken comparison and the exact details to give on the phone. You start it, and Buddy never calls anyone on its own.
