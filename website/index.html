How it works

From “Hey Buddy” to a route in one breath.

  1. Wake it up

    Say “Hey Buddy” or tap the avatar. The wake word is heard on the phone, so nothing leaves it until you speak to Buddy.

  2. Talk normally

    Buddy shows what it heard, so you know it got you right before it acts.

    You said: need a truck stop with showers

  3. Buddy picks and routes

    It weighs your truck, the distance and the parking, picks one place and sets the route. It answers in a sentence or two, out loud and on screen.

  4. Tap Go

    Drive with spoken turn-by-turn, or hand the trip to Google Maps or Waze.

Truck parking

It looks at the lot before you take the exit.

A listing saying “truck parking” isn’t the same as room for 70 feet of rig. Buddy checks satellite imagery of the place to judge whether a 53 ft tractor-trailer can park there, finds the truck entrance, and ends your route at it.

Breakdown help

Stopped on the shoulder? Say so. Buddy does the calling around.

Tell Buddy what happened. It asks at most one question, then reads reviews, hours and payment options while you deal with the truck. You get a short spoken comparison and the exact details to give on the phone. You start it, and Buddy never calls anyone on its own.
