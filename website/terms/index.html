The rules of the road for the app.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

    A posted clearance, weight limit, truck restriction or closure always wins over what the app says.

Who can use TruckBuddy

TruckBuddy is for professional drivers and people who work with them in the United States. You must be at least 18 years old.

Your account

  • You sign in with Apple or Google. TruckBuddy needs an account because your truck profile, history and trips are kept in it and follow you to every phone you sign in on.
  • Anyone signed in to your account can use TruckBuddy as you. If you lose a phone, use Your truck → Sign out of all phones on another one, or email us.
  • Access codes are for testers and app reviewers. They are personal, and we may turn a code off at any time.

Using the app while driving

  • Mount your phone and set it up before you drive. Talk to the copilot by voice; a single tap starts it.
  • Do not hold the phone, type or read long text while the vehicle is moving. Follow FMCSA rules and the laws of every state you drive in on the use of mobile phones.
  • Pull over to change settings, your truck profile or anything that takes more than one touch.

Routes, bridges and restrictions

  • Routes are planned for the height, length, weight and hazmat status in your truck profile. Keep it correct: a wrong height means a wrong route.
  • Clearance and restriction data come from map providers and public records. Not every bridge, tunnel, overhang or local restriction is in them. The app warns you about what it knows and cannot warn you about what it does not know.
  • If the app says it could not find a legal truck route, do not drive it without checking the restrictions yourself or getting a permit.

Parking, places, prices and hours of service

  • Parking checks, entrance directions and truck parking counts are estimates. A lot may be full, closed, private or not allowed for trucks.
  • Place details, phone numbers and fuel prices may be out of date. What drivers say about a place reflects other people’s opinions.
  • Hours-of-service clocks come from your ELD provider. Your logs and your ELD, not TruckBuddy, are the record that counts.
  • In a breakdown or emergency, call 911 or your carrier. TruckBuddy does not call for help.

Free service and limits

TruckBuddy is free while in early access. To keep it running, there is a daily limit on copilot questions; the map and routes keep working past it. Features may change, and the service may be unavailable at times.

Your content

You keep what you say to the copilot. You let us process it to answer you and to improve the service, as described in the Privacy Policy. Do not use the app to break the law, to harass anyone, or to overload, scrape or reverse engineer the service.

Data from others

Maps, places, weather and reviews in TruckBuddy come from third parties, including OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). That data belongs to them and is shown under their terms, with credits in the app and on its maps.
